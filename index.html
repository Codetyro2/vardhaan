FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you make informed decisions.

How long does the immigration process take?+
Processing times vary by country, visa category and individual application. Our team can explain the current pathway and documentation requirements.
What are the eligibility requirements for each country?+
Requirements depend on the pathway. We assess your profile, goals and documents before recommending relevant options.
Which documents do I need to apply?+
Typical requirements can include identity, education, employment, financial and supporting documents, depending on the visa.
Can I apply if I have a previous refusal?+
A previous refusal does not automatically answer whether a future application is possible; the refusal reason and new evidence need to be reviewed.
